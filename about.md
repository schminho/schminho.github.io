# About Me

## Product Owner & Developer

Creative, highly dependable, and exceptionally organized business allrounder specialized in **product and project management**, with an eye for detail and a strong sense of ownership. My work bridges the gap between **business and technology**, ensuring that products not only meet market demands but also leverage modern development frameworks and tools.

## Key Competencies
- **Effective Communicator** – Clearly translating business needs into actionable development tasks.
- **Leadership** – Driving teams towards strategic goals.
- **Requirements Engineering** – Defining and refining product features with precision.
- **Tech-Savvy** – Deeply engaged in software development and emerging tech.
- **Strong Work Ethic** – Delivering high-quality results consistently.
- **Contract Law & Financial Acumen** – Navigating agreements and budgets effectively.
- **Product Vision & Stakeholder Management** – Aligning cross-functional teams towards a shared goal.
- **Problem-Solving & Analytical Thinking** – Finding optimal solutions to complex challenges.
- **Strategic Mindset** – Planning and executing long-term success.

## Development Focus
Beyond my role as a **Product Owner**, I have a strong passion for **software development** and actively engage in coding using modern tools and frameworks:

- **Languages & Frameworks:** Python, JavaScript, React, Flutter
- **Styling & UI:** Tailwind CSS
- **Infrastructure & Deployment:** Render, Git
- **SaaS & Web Development:** Building scalable, user-friendly applications

This blend of **technical expertise and strategic leadership** allows me to not only **define great products** but also **contribute to their development** in meaningful ways.
